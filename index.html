How to Tell if a UK Online Casino is Safe

A slick website does not mean a trustworthy casino. Before you hand over any money, check these things:

  • Licensing: A proper licence from the UKGC, Malta Gaming Authority, or Curacao is the bare minimum. No licence means no protection if something goes wrong.
  • Bonus Terms That Make Sense: Some casinos advertise 50 free spins but attach 60x wagering. That means you would need to bet thousands before seeing any of your winnings. Look for sites with wagering between 10x and 40x.
  • How Fast They Pay: E-wallet withdrawals should land within 24 hours at any decent site. If a casino takes five days to process a PayPal payment, that tells you something.
  • What Other Players Say: Look for real reviews. If multiple people report held withdrawals or support that ignores them, find somewhere else.

Basswin Casino, Mystbet Casino, and Rollino Casino all claim proper licensing. Always check this yourself on the regulator's website before you deposit.
