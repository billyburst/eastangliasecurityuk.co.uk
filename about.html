About East Anglia Security UK

Your trusted partner in professional security services

Who We Are

East Anglia Security UK are a professional team of SITO trained contractors. We specialise in many areas of Security, including but not limited to, guardsmen, installing and repairing Security equipment and we also offer other facilities services.

All East Anglia Security UK contracts are tailor-made to meet the client's every detail. We pride ourselves on our flexibility, offering all levels of security services. The key executives are backed by a team of carefully vetted, fully trained and highly accredited security personnel to ensure that East Anglia Security UK delivers the same high standard of services 24/7.

All of our security personnel are industry standard SIA licensed and fully insured.
